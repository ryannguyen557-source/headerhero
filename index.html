How to play

Move

←→ or AD run.

↑ W Space jump. Hold for height.

Shift dash. E or L flick a nearby ball up.

P or Esc pause.

Headers

Hit the ball at the top of your jump for a peak header. Hold forward to drive it, back to lob it, down on the ground to cushion it.

↓ S in the air: dive header, fired at the goal.

Combos

Chain: three headers in a row without the ball touching the grass. Your next goal counts double, and the shot homes in on the goal.

Hit the very top of a jump for a perfect peak smash.

Rules

45 seconds. Most goals wins. A draw is a draw, but knockout ties go to a penalty shootout.

Every goal gets a replay. Click or press Space to skip it.

The notches on the stamina bar show what a dash and a dive cost. Q uses a legend's ability.

Arrows or A / D run. Up, W or Space jump. Shift dash. Down or S dive in the air. E flick. Q legend ability. P pause.
